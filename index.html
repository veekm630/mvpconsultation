The road to opening

Starting a pharmacy touches licensing boards, insurers, regulators, and vendors all at once. We've been through each step firsthand — and we'll walk you through them in the right order.

Step one

Plan & form the business

Entity structure, business plans, financing preparation, and a realistic startup budget before you commit a dollar.

Step two

Licenses & registrations

State board applications, NPI and NCPDP setup, DEA registration — and multi-state expansion when you're ready.

Step three

Payer & PBM credentialing

Insurance network enrollment, PSAO selection, and understanding reimbursement before you open your doors.

Step four

Operations & staffing

Workflow design, pharmacy software selection, hiring, and the policies and procedures that keep you compliant.

Step five

Specialty & niche services

Building beyond retail — specialty pharmacy, compounding, and clinical service lines that set you apart.

Step six

Marketing & first prescribers

Winning your first prescribers and patients, and growing script volume in a competitive market.
