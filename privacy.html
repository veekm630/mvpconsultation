Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

This policy describes how MVP Consulting ("we," "us") handles information collected through www.mvpconsulting.ai.

Information you provide

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you enter: your name, email address, phone number (if provided), and message. This information is transmitted through Formspree, a form-processing service, and delivered to our email inbox. We use it to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to provide our services.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics and Google Ads measurement tags on this site. These tools use cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as your device type, browser, pages visited, approximate location, and whether you arrived from one of our advertisements. We use this information to understand how the site is used and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. You can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How we use and share information

We use the information described above to respond to inquiries, provide and improve our services, and operate and measure our website and advertising. We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers that make this site work — such as Formspree (form processing) and Google (analytics and advertising measurement) — who process it on our behalf, or if required by law.

Cookies and your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. To opt out of personalized Google advertising, visit adssettings.google.com. To opt out of Google Analytics, you can install Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Data retention and your rights

We keep inquiry information as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above. To ask what information we hold about you, or to request correction or deletion, email us at hello@mvpconsulting.ai and we will respond promptly.

Changes and contact
